Questions

Do you bake gluten-free?

Yes: a gluten-free loaf on Fridays and almond cookies every day. They are baked in the same kitchen, so we cannot promise zero traces.

Can I order a cake?

Birthday and wedding cakes need three days. Use the order form or call us; we will confirm by phone.

Is there seating?

Twenty seats inside and six on the terrace in summer. Laptops are welcome before 11:00 and after 15:00.

Do you deliver?

Not yet. Orders are picked up at the counter; we keep them warm for 30 minutes.
